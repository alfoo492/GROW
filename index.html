Our journey is just beginning.

GROW – Grace Reaches On Wheels is a Tanzania-founded health initiative focused on helping underserved communities prevent and manage NCDs through culturally relevant education, technology, and community outreach. Since launching in May 2026, GROW has combined digital and AI-powered solutions with plans to expand into mobile health services and local partnerships through a sustainable hybrid model.

NCDs Awareness Library

Core health topics we educate on

🎗️ Cancer Awareness

Cancer is one of the leading NCDs. Early detection, screening, and open conversation save lives.

  • Recognise early warning signs: unexplained weight loss, persistent lumps, unusual bleeding
  • Regular screenings for breast, cervical, and prostate cancers are highly effective
  • Tobacco, alcohol, and poor diet are major modifiable risk factors
  • Family history increases risk — genetic counselling is available
  • Do not delay — persistent symptoms warrant immediate medical evaluation
❤️ Cardiovascular Disease

Heart disease and stroke are the leading cause of NCDs deaths globally — yet most cases are preventable through early intervention and lifestyle change.

  • Monitor blood pressure and cholesterol regularly
  • Reduce salt, saturated fats, and ultra-processed foods in your diet
  • Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate physical activity weekly
  • Avoid all forms of tobacco and limit alcohol intake
  • Manage chronic stress — it significantly impacts cardiovascular health
🩺 Diabetes

Type 2 diabetes is among the fastest-growing NCDs in sub-Saharan Africa. It is largely preventable and manageable with the right knowledge and tools.

  • Maintain a healthy body weight through balanced nutrition and activity
  • Limit refined carbohydrates, sugary beverages, and processed snacks
  • Test blood glucose regularly, especially with a family history of diabetes
  • Symptoms: excessive thirst, frequent urination, fatigue, blurred vision
  • Unmanaged diabetes leads to kidney failure, blindness, and amputation
🫁 Respiratory Disease

Chronic respiratory conditions such as asthma and COPD are exacerbated by indoor pollution, tobacco, and environmental factors prevalent in our region.

  • Eliminate tobacco use — the single most important preventive step
  • Transition to cleaner cooking fuels and improve household ventilation
  • Seek medical attention early for persistent cough, breathlessness, or wheezing
  • Annual lung function checks are recommended for high-risk individuals
🧠 Mental Health

Mental health is an integral and often overlooked dimension of non-communicable disease. Chronic illness significantly amplifies psychological burden.

  • Seek professional or peer support — early help leads to better outcomes
  • Strong community ties and social belonging are protective factors
  • Regular physical activity and adequate sleep substantially improve mental health
  • NCDs patients benefit greatly from integrated psychosocial support
🌿 Preventive Lifestyle

The majority of NCDs share a common set of modifiable risk factors. Sustained lifestyle change at the community level offers the highest return on health investment.

  • Adopt a diet rich in whole foods — vegetables, legumes, fruits, and grains
  • Stay hydrated and reduce dependence on sugary or processed drinks
  • Eliminate tobacco in all forms — cigarettes, shisha, and smokeless products
  • Prioritise consistent sleep and evidence-based stress management
  • Schedule annual preventive health check-ups without waiting for symptoms
